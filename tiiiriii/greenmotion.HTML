07 — GOOD QUESTIONS KNOW BEFORE YOU GO

Curious?
Good.

Electric mobility comes with questions. Here are a few worth understanding.

FAQ / 007
Are electric vehicles better for the environment? +

Battery-electric vehicles produce no tailpipe exhaust while driving. Their overall climate impact also depends on electricity generation, manufacturing, battery production, and lifetime use.

How long does it take to charge an EV? +

Charging time varies with battery size, starting charge, charger power, vehicle capability, and temperature. Home charging often takes hours, while compatible fast chargers can add energy much more quickly.

Are electric vehicles expensive to maintain? +

EVs generally have fewer moving powertrain components than petrol vehicles and don't require engine oil changes. Tyres, brakes, cabin filters, tyres, coolant systems, and other vehicle-specific maintenance still matter.

What affects electric vehicle range? +

Battery size, speed, temperature, terrain, driving style, vehicle load, heating, and air conditioning can all influence real-world range.

Can I charge an EV at home? +

Many owners charge at home using equipment suitable for their vehicle and electrical installation. Check local requirements and consult a qualified electrician where needed.
